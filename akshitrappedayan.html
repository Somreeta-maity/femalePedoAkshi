My Teacher, Akshi

Ms. Akshi is my favorite teacher. She has a way of explaining things that makes even the hardest lessons feel easy. Whenever I'm stuck on a problem, she doesn't just give me the answer — she asks questions that help me figure it out myself.

"You're capable of more than you think," she told the class on the first day. I remember it every time I feel like giving up.

She stays after class sometimes to help students who are struggling, and she always remembers the little things — like when I told her I was nervous about a test, she checked in with me the next day just to see how it went.

I want to be the kind of student who makes her proud, and someday, maybe the kind of teacher who inspires others the way she inspires me.

— A grateful student
